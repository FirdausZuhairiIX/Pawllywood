Inside the app, screen by screen

Seven parts that work together. Your pet profile sits at the centre, so a booking, a party and a poster all share the same details.

01

Home

Your pet's dashboard. Everything that matters today is one glance away, so you can book, join or create without hunting through menus.

  • Pet greeting and status : Your pet's photo and name, current membership tier and star points balance at the top of the screen.
  • What is next : A countdown card for your next resort stay, party or event, with quick links to view details or message the team.
  • Quick actions : Four one-tap shortcuts: Book a stay, Get event tickets, Make a poster and Show my member card.
  • Happening near you : Upcoming Club socials, dating nights and parties in your area, ordered by date, with member early-access labels.
  • Star moments feed : Fresh posters, event photos and community highlights, so the app feels like a red carpet and not a booking form.
  • Offers for you : Seasonal packages and member-only deals matched to your tier.
02

Pet profile

A digital passport for your cat. Fill it in once and every booking, event and poster picks it up automatically, so you never re-type the same details.

  • The basics : Name, photo, breed, colour, birthday and age. Add more than one pet and switch between them in a tap.
  • Personality and temperament : Shy, social or playful, plus what stresses them. This helps our team place them in the right supervised zone and choose suitable activities.
  • Care notes : Diet, allergies, medication schedule, favourite toys and anything the staff should know before a stay.
  • Health records : Upload vaccination certificates and get a reminder before they expire. Records are checked before a first visit.
  • Emergency contacts : Your backup contact and your regular vet, kept where staff can reach them fast.
  • Star gallery : Every photo from the Club and resort, every poster you make, and a shareable profile card for social media.
03

Events

The Club calendar. Find something to do with your pet, see what is coming up and lock in a ticket without leaving the app.

  • Calendar and filters : Browse by date, area and type: Club socials, pet dating nights, birthday premieres and brand collaborations.
  • Event details : Time, venue, what is included, capacity and the temperament level suited to each event, so shy cats are not thrown into a crowd.
  • Ticketing : Buy tickets in the app (dating and social events are RM80 to RM90 per pet). Tickets arrive as a QR code for check-in.
  • Pet dating opt-in : Choose whether your pet joins matchmaking events. Suggested matches are based on temperament and both owners must agree.
  • Member early access : VIP Paw and Pawllywood Royalty members see and book new events before they open to everyone.
  • Reminders and recap : Calendar sync, a reminder the day before and an event photo gallery posted afterwards.
04

Booking

One booking flow for every service, so a stay, a party and a Club visit all work the same way.

  • Choose a service : AURA Pet Resort stay (from RM180 a night), pet birthday party (from RM1,500), or a Club visit.
  • Pick dates and suites : Availability calendar, suite types and add-ons such as spa and grooming, with the price updating as you choose.
  • Health check gate : The app confirms vaccination records are valid before payment, so the shared space stays safe for every cat.
  • Party planner : Guest count, theme, cake flavour and photographer options, with a summary the team confirms with you.
  • Pay in the app : Member discounts applied automatically, a clear total and a receipt in your booking history.
  • During and after : Photo updates while your pet is with us, a check-out summary, easy rebooking and a way to leave feedback.
  • Manage changes : Reschedule or cancel from the booking page and chat with the guest experience team when you need a human.
05

Membership

Your Pawllywood card, in your pocket. See what your tier includes, use it at the door and upgrade whenever your pet's star power grows.

  • Digital member card : A QR card for entering the Club lounge and claiming perks, with your pet's name and tier on it.
  • Compare tiers : A side-by-side view of Paw Star, VIP Paw and Pawllywood Royalty so you can see exactly what each adds.
  • Upgrade any time : Move up a tier in a few taps. Club membership runs from RM49 to RM129 a month.
  • Perks tracker : Which benefits you have used this month, what is left and when they refresh.
  • Billing and renewal : Payment method, receipts, renewal date and a reminder before you are charged.
  • Digital platform access : Every tier includes the full app, and higher tiers unlock priority bookings and elite experiences.
06

AI movie poster generator

The Pet Movie Premiere studio. Turn a photo into a Hollywood-style poster in a few minutes. RM39 per poster, clearly labelled as AI-generated.

  • Upload : Pick a clear, well-lit photo. The app shows quick tips and warns you if the photo is too dark or your pet is hard to see.
  • Choose a genre : Pick a cinematic style such as drama, action, romance, fantasy or comedy, each with its own colours and lighting.
  • Personalise : Write the movie title, tagline and cast credit. The app suggests witty titles based on your pet's profile.
  • Preview and refine : See a few generated options, pick your favourite or ask for another take before you pay.
  • Download, print or share : Save in high resolution, order a printed poster, or post it with the #MyPetMyStar tag.
  • Your privacy : Photos are used only to create your poster, and every poster carries a small AI-generated label.
  • Poster collection : All your past posters live in your pet's star gallery, ready to reorder or share again.
07

Rewards

A thank-you that feels like stardom. Every visit, event and shared poster earns star points that turn into real perks.

  • Earn star points : Points for resort stays, event attendance, party bookings, sharing a poster and referring a friend.
  • Star levels : Climb from Rising Star to Headliner to Legend as your points grow. Higher levels earn points faster.
  • Redeem : Swap points for perks such as a free grooming add-on, event ticket credit, priority booking or Pawllywood merchandise.
  • Member boost : Higher membership tiers earn points faster, so upgrading pays back over time.
  • Referral code : Share your code and you and your friend both get a reward when they book.
  • Points history : A clear list of what you earned, what you spent and any points that are about to expire.
