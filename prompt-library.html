How we brief the AI.

Every Pawllywood visual starts with a carefully built prompt. Here are the prompts, the framework and the iterations behind our brand imagery.

Anatomy of a prompt

A powerful prompt has eight elements. Leave one out and the AI fills the gap with a guess.

  1. RoleDefine the AI's persona and expertise.
  2. TaskState the exact action or output needed.
  3. ContextProvide background and brand setting.
  4. InputDescribe subject details such as pet, style and mood.
  5. ConstraintsSet limits on format, tone and what to avoid.
  6. StyleSpecify aesthetic, lighting and composition.
  7. Output formatDeclare dimensions, file type and layout.
  8. Quality criteriaDefine what a great result looks like.

The Pawllywood prompt framework

A reusable structure for any Pawllywood image prompt, so every poster and photo feels like it came from the same studio.

WHO
Define the AI's role, for example a luxury brand photographer.
WHAT
Describe the task, for example generate a cinematic movie poster.
PET
Breed, colour, expression, costume or accessories.
WHERE
Environment and setting, for example a red carpet or velvet studio.
HOW
Style, lighting, composition and mood.
BRAND
Burgundy, gold and cream, with a glamorous and premium tone.
RULES
Constraints such as no watermarks and no blurry edges.
OUTPUT
Format, resolution and intended use.
