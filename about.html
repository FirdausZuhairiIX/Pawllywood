Care, community and content under one brand.

Pawllywood is a luxury pet lifestyle company based in the Klang Valley, Malaysia.

Pet owners should not need five different providers to give their pets a five-star life.

Boarding is functional, not luxurious. Pets have few safe ways to socialise. Celebrations have no dedicated venue. Owners have nowhere to meet each other. We built Pawllywood to close all four gaps.

The idea is simple. Modern owners treat their pets as family and want memorable experiences, not only products. At the heart of Pawllywood is our Club, a members' lounge where cat owners socialise and network while their cats relax in supervised zones. Around it sit the AURA Pet Resort, pet birthday parties, dating and Club events, pet influencer management and AI-generated pet movie posters.

Our mission

To give pets a five-star life and their owners a community to enjoy it with.

Our vision

To become Malaysia's most loved pet lifestyle brand, expanding from the Klang Valley to Johor Bahru and Penang.

What we stand for

Five values guide every experience we design.

  • Pet wellbeing firstComfort, consent cues and suitable activities guide every experience.
  • Playful creativityWe turn familiar pet moments into surprising stories and celebrations.
  • Thoughtful luxuryPolished service and details that feel special without losing warmth.
  • Trust and transparencyWe explain care standards, inclusions and AI-created content clearly.
  • CommunityWe help owners connect through shared experiences while respecting each pet's temperament.
