Questions we get asked

How do I join the Club?

Choose a membership tier: Paw Star, VIP Paw or Pawllywood Royalty. Club membership runs from RM49 to RM129 a month, and you can upgrade anytime. An event ticket at RM80 to RM90 is an easy way to try the Club first.

How do you keep cats safe and calm?

Cats relax in supervised zones with trained staff, vaccination checks and controlled capacity, so shared spaces stay calm.

Are the movie posters made by AI?

Yes. Our AI creates each poster from your pet's photo, and we always label AI-generated content clearly.

Will you open outside the Klang Valley?

That is the plan. We start in the Klang Valley, then expand to Johor Bahru and Penang.
