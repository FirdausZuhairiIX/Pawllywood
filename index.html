What is Pawllywood?

Pawllywood is a luxury pet lifestyle company based in the Klang Valley, Malaysia. We bring together care, community and content under one brand, so pet owners no longer need five different providers.

Boarding is functional, not luxurious. Pets have few safe ways to socialise. Celebrations have no dedicated venue. Owners have nowhere to meet each other. We built Pawllywood to close all four gaps.
